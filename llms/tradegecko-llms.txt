# TradeGecko

> TradeGecko was a Singapore-based cloud inventory and order management platform for SMEs, wholesalers, and online retailers, launched in 2013 by Carl Thompson and Cameron and Bradley Priest, with integrations into Shopify, WooCommerce, and Xero. Intuit acquired TradeGecko in August 2020 (reported at more than US$80 million) and rebranded it QuickBooks Commerce; the standalone platform and its public REST API were subsequently discontinued. As of 2026-07-21 api.tradegecko.com and developer.tradegecko.com no longer resolve, and tradegecko.com is no longer operated by the company (it now serves an unrelated content site).
